A generative video experiment built around the moments where a model fills in the world *beyond* its training — doors where there are no walls, text that reads as letters but is not language.

The interest is not the failure. It is the *confidence*. These artifacts insist on their reality, which is exactly what makes them worth showing. Sources, processing pipeline, and the prompts that produced the weirdest frames are kept in the repo (`nasa-hallucination-video/`) as a methods note.
