The cards you have pictured come from several different classic 1990s Marvel trading card sets. Here is a breakdown of each set's overall value, along with the specific cards or inserts from those sets that hold real value today:

---

### 1. 1993 SkyBox Marvel Universe Series IV (Series 4)
*Pictured: **Dr. Strange (#65)***

*   **Base Cards Value:** Very low. Like most 90s sets, these cards were mass-produced. Common base cards like this Dr. Strange are generally worth **$0.50 to $2** in raw (ungraded) condition.
*   **What to Look For (Chase Cards):**
    *   **Spider-Man vs. Venom Hologram (H-IV):** This is the most sought-after card in the set (inserted 1 in every 180 packs). It comes in 5 color variations (Teal, Blue/Navy, Red, Green, and Green/Gold). Raw copies go for **$40 to $100+** depending on color and condition; high-grade graded copies (PSA 10) can fetch **$300 to $800+**.
    *   **Red Foil 2099 Insert Set:** A 9-card subset (inserted 1 in 10 packs). Individual cards are worth **$3 to $10**.

---

### 2. 1994 Fleer Ultra X-Men
*Pictured: **Jean Grey (#5)***

*   **Base Cards Value:** Raw base cards are generally worth **$0.50 to $3** depending on the character.
*   **What to Look For (Chase Cards):**
    *   **Silver X-Overs (Walmart Exclusives):** These are extremely rare alternative prints of the base cards that were only found in special Walmart packs. Unlike the base versions, they feature shiny silver foil stock and special numbering on the back. These can regularly fetch **$50 to $150+** each, even ungraded.
    *   **Fatal Attractions Insert Set:** These etched-foil inserts go for **$3 to $10** raw.
    *   **Team Portraits:** A 9-card puzzle insert set with art by the Hildebrandt brothers. Individually they are worth **$2 to $5**.

---

### 3. 1992 SkyBox Marvel Universe Series III (Series 3)
*Pictured: **Silver Surfer (#34)** and **Epoch (#105)***

*   **Base Cards Value:** Very low. Individual raw base cards are typically **$0.50 to $2**. 
*   **What to Look For (Chase Cards):**
    *   **Holograms (Venom, Spider-Man, Wolverine, Hulk, Ghost Rider):** Inserted 1 in every 12 packs. The **Venom hologram (H-4)** is the most valuable and can sell for **$20 to $50** raw (graded PSA 10 copies have sold for **$200+**).
    *   **Checklist Variation:** Look closely at the final checklist card (#200). If it has a solid "white box" on the top and bottom of the back, it is a rarer variant worth **$10 to $20**.

---

### 4. 1991 SkyBox Marvel Universe Series II (Series 2)
*Pictured: **Fantastic Four vs Skrulls (#103)***

*   **Base Cards Value:** Very low, typically **$0.50 to $2** raw.
*   **What to Look For (Chase Cards):**
    *   **Holograms (Spider-Man, Wolverine, Hulk, Doctor Doom, Magneto):** Inserted 1 in every 10 packs. The **Hulk (H-2)** and **Spider-Man (H-1)** holograms are highly desired, selling for **$15 to $45** raw and **$100 to $300+** in graded gem-mint condition.

---

### Summary Advice
If you are sorting through a larger collection of these:
1. **Look for Holograms and Foil Inserts:** Keep an eye out for shiny/holographic cards, as those are the "hits" from these boxes.
2. **Condition is Everything:** If you have cards of iconic characters (Spider-Man, Wolverine, Venom) in flawless, pack-fresh condition (flawless corners, edges, and centering), they might be candidates for professional grading, which is the main way standard base cards become valuable today.

*Pictured: **"Twisted Terrorists!" (#85)***

*   **Graded Cards:** Perfect PSA 10 graded copies of key characters (like Michael Keaton's Batman or Jack Nicholson's Joker) can sell for **$40 to $80**, but raw common cards are not worth the cost of grading.

### 2. 1991 Impel G.I. Joe Series 1
*Pictured: **Duke (#2)***

*   **What to Look For:** If you have any G.I. Joe cards that look similar but feature **shiny gold borders**, those are the rarer *1992 G.I. Joe Hall of Fame* inserts that came packaged with action figures. Those hold a bit more individual value depending on the character.

---

### 3. 1989 Topps Ghostbusters II
*Pictured: **"At The Museum" (#22)***

*   **What to Look For:** Authentic autographed cards (from actors like Dan Aykroyd, Annie Potts, etc.) certified by PSA/DNA can sell for hundreds of dollars, but the base movie stills are very common.

### 4. 1991 Topps Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves
*Pictured: **"Battling Little John!" (#21)***

---

### 5. 1991 Impel Terminator 2: Judgment Day

*   **Complete Sets:** A complete 140-card set trades online for **$15 to $25**.

### 1. 1990 Impel Marvel Universe Series I Hologram (Spider-Man)
*Pictured: **Spider-Man Hologram (#MH-1)***

*   **Value:** **This is by far the most valuable card shown in your photos so far.** Because this hologram surface is extremely prone to hairline scratches, finding one in pristine condition is very rare.
    *   **Ungraded / Raw:** Standard copies in decent condition sell for **$15 to $35**.
    *   **Near Mint to Mint (PSA 9):** Professionally graded copies fetch **$150 to $250+**.
    *   **Gem Mint (PSA 10):** If there are absolutely no surface scratches, perfect corners, and flawless centering, a PSA 10 copy is highly sought after by collectors and can sell for **$5,000 to $10,000+** at auction due to its low population. 

---
