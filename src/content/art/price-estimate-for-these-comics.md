Professional grading (CGC/CBCS) generally costs **$35 to $50+ per comic** (when factoring in shipping, handling, and insurance). Therefore, a comic is only worth grading if the value jump in a **9.8 (Near Mint/Mint)** grade significantly exceeds the cost of the grading itself.
