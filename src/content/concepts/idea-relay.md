**One line:** Kickstarter funds a creator; the Idea Relay *hands off* the idea itself — with provable credit for every contribution along the lineage.

**The core inversion:** the asset isn't the creator, it's the idea's lineage. Posting is free and explicitly not a funding ask; money flows in later, downstream, when a relayed project graduates to funding or subscription, and flows back proportionally to score.

**Mechanics:**

1. Originator posts concept/prototype + declared blockers (skill / time / funds / tools / drive)
2. Overlap engine finds related projects, proposes best path: merge / handoff / consolidate — with transparent match logic
3. Blockchain ledger anchors the original post and every contribution as signed transactions → lineage DAG, provable priority
4. Contributions earn weighted value-added scores (downstream builds, adoption, endorsements)
5. Royalty pool on the downstream event splits by score weight; originator holds a reserved floor

**Open problems:** attribution of value, license defaults for forks, Sybil resistance, cold start (two-sided network), securities-law risk on revenue-sharing tokens.

**Why it's on the site:** the framing is the deliverable — ideas as relayable assets, credit as provenance, royalties as arithmetic on the lineage.
