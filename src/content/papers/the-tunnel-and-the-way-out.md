## The Tunnel and the Way Out

This collection of posts represents my journey through the dark years of opioid use and recovery. It is not a story of triumph over adversity but rather an unvarnished look at how one navigates from the depths to the light.

### What Is Working?
Working means being honest about what happened, why it happened, and most importantly, what actually helped me get out. This isn't a tale of redemption; it’s a pragmatic exploration of survival strategies that proved effective in real-world conditions.

### Grouping Member Posts into Narrative Arcs
The member posts are scattered across different sections on this site—projects, garden notes, blog entries—but they all contribute to understanding my path through addiction:
- **Projects** like 'A Plain of Jars' Bible document the memoir serialization process, which is both an act of reflection and catharsis.
- Blog entries from 2026 detail research into legitimate online work opportunities and budget considerations for local LLM servers, indicating efforts towards financial stability post-recovery.
- Art pieces such as animated video ideas reflect a shift towards creative outlets that don't involve substance use.
- Garden notes cover technical aspects ranging from setting up Whisper (a voice-to-text tool) to creating Kickstarter campaigns, showing how practical steps can lead to tangible outcomes in sobriety.

### Current State & What Comes Next
I am currently documenting my recovery journey through various mediums—writing, art, and technology projects. Each piece is part of building a new life without the crutch of opioids or any other substances. The next phase involves solidifying these practices into sustainable routines and sharing them openly with others who might be navigating similar challenges.
