## The Pipeline That Captured My Father

This piece is about something I've been tracking for years: how my dad went from a man with dreams of personal growth to someone ensnared in an MLM (Multi-Level Marketing) scheme. It's part memoir, part analysis, all truth.

### What Is 'The Machine'?
The machine refers to the self-help-to-extraction pipeline where individuals start off seeking betterment and end up exploited. This isn't just theoretical; it's deeply personal. In this context, 'the machine' encompasses everything from motivational seminars to pyramid schemes that masquerade as legitimate business opportunities.

### The Goal
I want people inside these systems—like my father—to see what’s happening clearly. I’m not here to mock or belittle those who are caught up in such cycles but rather to provide a clear-eyed view of how they operate so others can avoid similar fates.

#### A Plain of Jars: Project Bible & Story Addendum
'A Plain of Jars' is the memoir-in-three-volumes project where adult me travels back through time to warn and orient young JD against pitfalls he encountered growing up. Volume one covers his childhood, volume two delves into his underground years (ska bands, punk shows), while volume three details returning home after recovery from addiction. The 'MLM Truth Project,' an addendum to this series, specifically addresses my father's journey within MLM schemes.

#### OpenCode Zen vs Claude Code
Understanding that not all open-source models are created equal helps demystify why certain projects succeed or fail based on their underlying philosophies. This insight applies directly to how self-help movements can be hijacked for profit under the guise of community benefit.

### Current State & Next Steps
Currently, I'm developing content strategies like those outlined in 'The Light Field' Substack serialization and a YouTube channel dedicated to documenting these processes ('YouTube Process Channel'). These channels aim to educate viewers about the mechanics behind such pipelines while also providing practical advice on walking away from them gracefully (as detailed in papers like 'I Automated My Creative Brain Because I Had To') and understanding financial pitfalls ('Kickstarter Financial Model').

#### Propagandhi Album Analysis
Proposing an analysis-driven approach through music videos might seem tangential but it’s part of building relatable narratives that resonate with audiences. By breaking down complex ideas into digestible media formats, we can reach more people effectively.

### Unresolved Questions & Future Work
The biggest unresolved question is how deeply entrenched these systems are within communities where they originate. How do you dismantle a system when its very existence seems to be built on the backs of those who believe in it? The answer lies somewhere between education and community support structures designed to help individuals navigate out safely without falling back into similar traps.
