There is no direct train from Los Angeles to Vancouver, BC, so you will need to take two separate Amtrak trains, transferring in Seattle. 

The total cost for a one-way **coach ticket** typically ranges from **$200 to $450**, depending on how far in advance you book and the time of year. Here is the breakdown:

1. **Los Angeles to Seattle (Amtrak Coast Starlight):**
   * **Coach Price:** ~$127 to $300+
   * **Details:** This is a scenic 35-hour ride up the coast. Private sleeper rooms are also available but they are significantly more expensive, often pushing the total trip cost well over $700. 

2. **Seattle to Vancouver, BC (Amtrak Cascades):**
   * **Coach Price:** ~$70 to $150
   * **Details:** A 4-hour scenic journey connecting Seattle to Vancouver's Pacific Central Station. 

**Tips for getting the best price:**
* **Book early:** Prices are dynamic and increase as the departure date approaches. Try to book at least 3-4 weeks in advance on the official [Amtrak website](https://www.amtrak.com/).
* **Check discounts:** Amtrak offers everyday discounts for seniors (10%), students (15%), children 2-12 (50%), and military personnel (10%).
* **Bring your passport:** Since you are crossing the border into Canada, you will need a valid passport!
