The habit: every Thursday, one new venture idea gets the full treatment — one-line restatement, 3–5 highest-leverage actions, top risk named. By Monday it's either funded into the queue, merged with an existing project, or killed with a written reason.

Why Thursday: the rest of the week is for building, and the idea gets a weekend to either grow legs or die quietly.

Why written reasons: killing an idea without documenting why is how you re-invent it six months later with the same flaws.
