Most agent setups are chat in, chat out. TERAX is different: its instructions demand a living workspace — `business-plan.md`, `research/` with sources, `models/` with unit-economics tables, `campaign/` with copy and assets.

The insight: **the artifact is the memory.** A conversation evaporates; a folder of labeled, sourced files persists and compounds. Every idea tested leaves receipts behind.

Corollary: when an idea fails, the failure is *captured* — the research folder, the teardown, the reason. The graveyard of finished experiments becomes the portfolio.
