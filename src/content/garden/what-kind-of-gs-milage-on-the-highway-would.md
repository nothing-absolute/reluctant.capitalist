what kind of gs milage on the highway would 1976 winnebago chevy get?

a 1976 winnebago built on a chevrolet chassis (typically the chevrolet p30 chassis with a 350 or 454 v8 carbureted engine) generally gets between 6 and 8 miles per gallon (mpg) on the highway.

### key factors affecting the mileage:
* **cruising speed:** because these motorhomes have the aerodynamics of a large box, driving faster than 55–60 mph will drastically drop your fuel economy. keeping your speed moderate is the best way to stay closer to the 8 mpg mark.
* **vehicle load:** a fully loaded rig with fresh water, gear, and passengers will pull the mileage down closer to 6 mpg (or lower on hilly terrain).
* **engine maintenance:** because these vintage engines rely on carburetors, keeping the spark plugs clean, the carburetor properly tuned, and the tires inflated correctly is critical to maintaining decent fuel efficiency.
