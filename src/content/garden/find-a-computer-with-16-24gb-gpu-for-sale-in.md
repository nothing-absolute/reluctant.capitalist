### The Hardware Reality for LTX 2.5 on Mac
Because LTX 2.5 is a massive 22-Billion parameter video generation model, it is incredibly demanding. On Apple computers, you do not have a dedicated graphics card; instead, the CPU and GPU share "Unified Memory." 
*   **A 16GB or 24GB Mac will NOT be enough.** If you try to run local inference for LTX 2.5 on a Mac with 16GB of RAM via the MLX framework, it will likely crash or fail to load because the model weights alone can take up ~42GB of space in memory.
*   **The Minimum Requirement:** You need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, or M4) with an absolute minimum of **32GB of Unified Memory**.

## what year did Apple switch to M1 instead of intel?

Apple announced the transition from Intel processors to their own Apple Silicon (starting with the **M1** chip) in **June 2020** at their Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC). 

The very first computers featuring the M1 chip—the MacBook Air, the 13-inch MacBook Pro, and the Mac mini—were released to the public later that year in **November 2020**. 

If you are looking at used Macs for running LTX 2.5, you will want to make sure the model is from **Late 2020 or newer** and explicitly says it has an "Apple M1" (or M2, M3, M4) chip rather than an Intel Core i5, i7, or i9.
