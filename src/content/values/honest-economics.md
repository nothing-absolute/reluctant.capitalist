title: "Honest economics"
description: "Numbers with sources beat narratives with confidence. Every claim is either cited, estimated, or flagged as an assumption."
weight: 9
date: 2026-08-01
tags: ["values", "economics"]
---

If a plan only works in a spreadsheet with forgiving assumptions, it doesn't work. Say what a thing costs, show the math, label the guesses. The math always balances — or the plan is wrong.
