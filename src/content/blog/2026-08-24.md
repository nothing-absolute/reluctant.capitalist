00:15 - xTool O1 Omni for printed needlepoint canvases

Verdict: workable but not ideal. Flatbed UV (330×420mm bed, ±0.2mm vision system) can print on 13/18-mesh canvas, but UV ink cures as a rigid layer that can crack/flake when flexible mesh bends during stitching/blocking; heavy coverage risks bridging the open mesh holes. Dual-Head edition's flexible white ink would help adhesion on flexible substrates. Better-fit alternatives for this niche: sublimation onto polyester mesh canvas (cheap, known to work), or hand-painting with acrylics.
