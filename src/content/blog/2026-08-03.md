**Small-Scale Biodiesel Refinery Costs (Proof of Concept)**
- DIY build (Appleseed/water-heater reactor): ~$150–$2,000; typical home setup under $1,000
- Murphy's Machines plans: ~$300–$2,000 in materials
- Turnkey automated (BioPro 150/190/380): ~$6,400–$36,000
- Production cost: ~$0.85–$1.25/gal excluding feedstock; free restaurant UCO + ~$1/gal → competitive vs diesel
- Feedstock: used cooking oil often free; ~1 gal oil → ~1 gal biodiesel; glycerol byproduct salable (soap)
- Safety: methanol is toxic/flammable, lye caustic, quality testing needed (ASTM D6751/EN 14214)
- Note: biodiesel runs in DIESEL engines, not gas engines
- Upcycled/scrap PoC build estimate: reactor (free water heater or used IBC tote $50–$150) + pump (~$50) + valves/plumbing (~$80) + wash tank (~free–$50) + heat element (~$30) + first chemicals (10 gal methanol + lye, ~$60) + filters/safety (~$50) → ~$300–$800 all-in
- Throughput: ~40–50 gal/batch for a 55-gal drum or water-heater reactor; ~1–3 days/batch (1–2 hr reaction at 55–60°C, overnight settle, then washing/drying); typical cadence is 1 batch/week → ~200 gal/mo
- Footprint: equipment itself ~8 x 4 ft (reactor, wash, settling drums at ~2 ft dia each); with clearance + feedstock/fuel storage fits in ~10 x 10 ft to 10 x 12 ft — corner of a 2-car garage / about one parking space
- Diesel E-450 Class C RV highway mpg: ~10–13 typical (7.3L PSD ~10–13, 6.0L ~12–13); gas V10 gets 8–10. One 40–50 gal batch at ~12 mpg ≈ 500–600 miles of RV travel
