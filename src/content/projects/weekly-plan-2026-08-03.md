#### 🔁 Rollover Rules

- **Big task** → moves to next day automatically. No guilt, no penalty. Skipped 2+ days in a row → either breaks into micro-chunks or goes to a weekend flow slot.
- **Started big task but didn't finish?** Write the *saved state* line (where you are + the next 5-minute action). Resume picks up from there instantly.
- **Micro-task** → rolls over, and Guardian features it first when it catches you scattered.
- **Rolled over twice** → promoted to top of tomorrow's list + Guardian's featured suggestion.

#### 🤖 Refocus Guardian (the notification)

Built this week in `Prototypes/focus-guardian/`. When browser/desktop activity looks scattered:

1. **Full-screen interruption** (intrusive on purpose, no lockouts).
2. Asks: *How are you feeling? Is this work — part of a project, just fun/entertainment, or distraction from a feeling/someone?*
3. If distraction → offers today's easiest micro-task to rebuild the reward loop.
4. Shows past wins: "Last Tue you stopped a scroll, finished the survey draft — that felt good." Later: plays a **win clip** you recorded after finishing a task.
5. **No time limits, no blocking.** Lockouts only exist as an *opt-in* extreme mode (e.g. 4+ hr porn/binge streak, or hours of "how do I code X" without picking a roadmap).
