One of the venture experiments from the Thursday idea-test cycle. The honest summary: tested, found the wedge harder than expected, paused with intent.

**What was validated:** the operator problem is real and people pay for coordination pain — the adjacent market research held up.

**What failed:** the specific wedge required a network effect we couldn't bootstrap alone, and the acquisition math didn't close for an indie budget.

**What survived:** the research discipline. Every experiment leaves behind a `research/` folder, a teardown of the failed assumption, and a sharper definition of what we will not build.

Paused, not killed. The pattern is the product.
