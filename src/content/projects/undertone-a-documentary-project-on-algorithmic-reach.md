## What is Undertone? A Documentary Project on Algorithmic Reach
Undertone is an ambitious documentary project aimed at demystifying the complex world of algorithms. The goal is to create compelling content that not only informs but also empowers audiences by shedding light on how these invisible forces shape our digital interactions.
### Goal: Expose and Educate
Our primary objective with Undertone is straightforward yet profound—unveil the mechanisms behind algorithmic reach, making it accessible for everyone. By doing so, we aim to foster a more informed user base capable of navigating their online experiences with greater awareness.
#### Current State: Building Blocks in Place
The project has several components already underway:
- **Documentary Series**: We are developing scripts and concepts for short documentaries (around 10 minutes each) focusing on different aspects of algorithms' impact on society.
- **Interactive Content**: Projects like 'OpenCode Zen' explore open-source models that challenge proprietary systems.
- **Social Campaigns**: Leveraging platforms such as Instagram and YouTube, we're creating ad campaigns designed to reach wide audiences effectively.
### Narrative Arc
**Phase One - Conceptualization & Scriptwriting:**
The journey began with brainstorming sessions where various ideas were generated. These ranged from examining the psychological effects of algorithmic recommendation systems to exploring how these technologies influence our purchasing decisions. Scripts are being written based on these concepts (e.g., Video 3: The Algorithm's Mind Control).
**Phase Two - Visualizing Concepts:**
Next came visual elements—thumbnails for videos, animated clips showcasing real-world impacts, and even a proposed 'Guardian Kids Phone' campaign aimed at educating younger viewers about digital safety.
**Phase Three - Implementation Plans:**
Detailed implementation plans have been drafted covering everything from renting out an RV for road trips that document local stories impacted by algorithms to refining Kickstarter campaigns designed around open-source projects like OpenPlotter or the Lumina Phone.
### Future Steps
The next phase involves executing these detailed plans. This includes creating visually exciting content through tools such as Remotion Video Ads and ensuring our social media strategies are robust enough to reach diverse audiences effectively. We will also continue iterating based on feedback received during beta testing phases of various components within Undertone.
#### What is Unresolved?
The biggest challenge remains in balancing educational depth with accessibility—ensuring complex topics remain engaging without oversimplifying them. Additionally, securing funding consistently throughout this multi-phase project requires careful planning and execution.
