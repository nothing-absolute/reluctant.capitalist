### YouTube Pedal Demo Channel — AI-Generated Guitar Parts

#### The Core Idea
A YouTube channel that demos guitar effects pedals, synthesizers, drum machines, and MIDI kits — but instead of the creator playing, a **local LLM generates the demo audio**. The LLM is fine-tuned to clone the "soul" of each effect from existing reviews, making the demo parts accurate and musical.

#### Why This Works
- Most demo channels rely on the host's playing skill — this removes that bottleneck
- Local LLM means no API costs, full privacy, full creative control
- Scales across instruments: guitar, synth, drum machines, e-drums
- Niche community = high engagement, loyal audience

#### Phases

##### Phase 1: Validation (Niche Makers → Referral Links)
- Target small-batch / boutique effect pedal makers
- Offer: "I'll demo your pedal with AI-generated parts that capture its sound"
- Revenue: referral/affiliate links from their store
- Goal: 5-10 demos, prove the concept works, build initial audience

##### Phase 2: Growth (Sponsorships)
- Use Phase 1 metrics (views, engagement, conversion) to approach mid-size makers
- Target the ones with highest revenue potential first
- Offer: sponsored demo slots, product placement, series sponsorship
- Revenue: flat fee + commission

##### Phase 3: Living Wage + Community
- Prove the channel generates enough for basic living expenses
- Transition focus to building a network/community of like-minded musicians, makers, and tinkerers
- Outreach to people aligned with values (open source, local tech, anti-corporate)

#### Target Niche Makers (Phase 1 Candidates)
- Small-batch boutique pedal builders
- Local/regional effect makers
- Open-source hardware projects
- Makers on Reverb, Etsy, or direct-sale

#### Revenue Model
| Phase | Source | Est. Monthly |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Affiliate/referral (5-10% commission) | $200-800 |
| 2 | Sponsored spots ($500-2000/video) | $2,000-8,000 |
| 3 | Community + diversified (merch, patreon, consulting) | $3,000-5,000+ |

#### The Deeper Why
This isn't just a business — it's a way to:
- Build a life aligned with personal values
- Create a network of like-minded people
- Prove that meaningful, values-driven work can sustain itself
- Escape the conventional job track that feels like a slow death
