Artist Partnership — Term Sheet Draft (A Plain of Jars)

*Pre-negotiation draft, 2026-09-19. Written to be sent to an artist as the starting document, or used as the interview/pressure-test for an artist candidate. Not legal advice — review by an entertainment attorney (~$1-2k) is a rounding error vs. what it protects.*

**The one clause this rides on:** a precisely *defined* Net Revenue. If the artist pushes back on the definition, the deal will die in the accounting, not the art. Everything else is negotiable; that definition is the floor.

#### 1. Works & parties
- JD (writer/producer) commissions the Artist (illustrator) for *A Plain of Jars, Book One*, ~180 finished pages: roughs allowed per agreed workflow, finished sequential art in the agreed style (B&W w/ tone, consistent with series), cover(s), and any campaign art (samples, KS banner) credited separately or in-kind per negotiation.
- Artist is an independent contractor (1099), not an employee. Each side pays own taxes; no benefits.
- Letters/design/color: separate line item paid as a production cost (not from page rate) [per bible: ~$3.5k/volume].

#### 2. Compensation
**2.1 Page-rate floor (guaranteed).** $150/page, paid on approved finished pages. Payment cadence: milestone-based, e.g. 30% on signing (advance against pages 1-20), then per approved block of 20 pages, 10% retained until final print-ready delivery. Advance is debt until worked off; artist keeps advance if JD terminates for convenience (see §5).

**2.2 Net Revenue share (the real money).** Artist receives **[X]%** (recommend 45%) of **Net Revenue**, defined as:
> Gross receipts from the Work (Kickstarter receipts after platform fees; retail/direct/convention/digital sales) **minus** production costs — printing, shipping/fulfillment, pledge manager fees, packaging, marketing/advertising, design/lettering, ebook conversion, and taxes (income & KS backup withholding), accounted per the published budget. "Gross" excludes art originals sales, which are 100% artist (with JD receiving no cut unless co-sold).

- Share paid quarterly, with 30-day statement in writing (sheet with columns: gross, deductions, artist share, JD share).
- An audit right: either side may, at own cost, review the other's records annually; prevailing party on a material discrepancy (>5%) recovers audit cost. *This clause is the trust mechanism — keep it.*

**2.3 What the share yields [estimates] — the honest range:**
| Campaign | Net after costs | Artist 45% share | Artist total w/ $27k |
|---|---|---|---|
| $50k (target) | ~$1-2k | ~$500-900 | ~$27.5-27.9k |
| $70k | ~$15-18k | ~$7-8k | ~$34-35k |
| $100k | ~$38-42k | ~$17-19k | ~$44-46k |
| Plus retail yr-1 (500-2k copies, $8-12 net) | ~$4-24k | ~$2-11k | adds on |

**2.4 Conventions/originals.** At shows, artist sells originals 100% to self; if JD is present and co-selling sketches, agree splits on a table-by-table basis before each show. Nothing in this agreement prevents the artist from selling services to other clients.

#### 3. Rights & ownership
| Asset | Owned by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Story, text, characters, series bible | JD | Includes "the 15," Nick, Road Home arc, title |
| Finished page art (reproduction rights during term) | JD (licensed from artist) | For the Work + marketing of the Work only |
| Physical original pages | Artist (100%) | JD may request scans; artist may not sell pages that would expose the story before publication |
| Published work copyright | Joint or JD-owned per jurisdiction | Get attorney advice; a single-copyright-holder structure is cleaner for adaptations |
| Ancillary (film/TV/audio/adaptation) | JD owns; artist gets [25-50%] of JD's net on adaptations of the GN | Cap in part: the story is adaptive without the art; keeps the art affordable |
| Portfolio/promo | Artist | Artist may show finished pages, post samples, cite the book; embargo until N months after V1 launch [negotiable] |
| Kickstarter/trailer/etc. | JD-produced; artist credited | -- |

#### 4. V2 & V3 — options, not obligations
- On successful funding of V1, JD holds an **option** to commission V2 on the same terms, with page rate **renegotiated in good faith or +[$10-25]/page** per volume [recommend indexing to the campaign's funding level: funding >$70k → top of band]. V3 mirrors V2.
- Artist has **right of first refusal** on V2/V3 (can decline; JD then free to engage another artist for future volumes — original-art ownership and "the 15" continuity handled per §3).
- Neither side is obligated. This is what keeps both of your off-ramps open.

#### 5. Off-ramps & exit terms
- **JD terminates without cause:** artist keeps fees for completed approved pages + $$ on any work-in-progress block (kill fee ≈ 25% of the in-progress milestone) + keeps the signing advance. JD may finish the volume with another artist; pages JD paid for transfer to JD for finishing, with artist credited "pencils/inks by [Artist]" on those pages.
- **Artist terminates:** JD owns all pages paid for (reproduction rights as §3); no kill fee owed; artist may not use those pages commercially except portfolio; JD may complete with a replacement artist. A modest **re-coupment** if artist quits mid-volume after large advance [defensible if advance > delivered work value].
- **Abandonment:** if JD takes no active production step for 24 consecutive months (no campaign, no release) the artist's option obligations lapse; artist may request in writing to finish/sell pages freely. Series-pause/dormant-newsletter does not trigger this — define "abandonment" precisely.
- **IP separation:** on any exit, each party keeps their pre-agreement property (artist: originals & portfolio; JD: story & characters, who may be illustrated by another hand thereafter).

#### 6. Timeline & deliverables [per bible pacing]
- Production: V1 art in 9-24 months; delivery cadence ~5-10 pages/wk; script pages locked 2 weeks before drawing each block.
- Revisions: 2 rounds per page included; beyond that, negotiated rate. JD gives line-edit notes in writing.
- Palette/style reference locked by the 2 sample pages at signing (this is the W10 "artist sample" gate).
- Slip schedule: launch/no-launch dates follow the 12-wk Substack → KS calendar; V1 ship ~12-18 months after campaign.

#### 7. Credit & promotion
- Cover: "Written by JD · Illustrated by [Artist]" (or agreed order). Interior copyright page credits artist's full name + links.
- Both sides may reference the project for promotion; neither claims sole authorship.
- Artist gets concertina/launch appearances, signed copies, and a "creator tier" on KS (e.g., 3-5 artist-original sketch rewards where artist keeps 100% of that tier's net) [negotiable — commonly a catalyst for overfunding].

#### 8. Process rules (from the series bible — non-negotiable)
- **Names are changed. Everything that matters is true.** — applies to the whole series, including the art (no embellishing settings into recognizable real people/places in libelous ways; Rick Steves is fictionalized; "the 15" anonymized in art — no likenesses).
- Content notes on trauma chapters (04/05/06) shipped in the book front-matter; JD writes them.
- If it doesn't sound like JD it doesn't ship — JD has final story say; artist has final art say for craft/quality callbacks. Define a 2-person escalation (mediation) if a page is disputed.

#### 9. Legal
- Mediation (non-binding) before any litigation; small claims carve-out. Governing law: South Dakota. No assignment by either side without consent. Entire agreement; amendments in writing signed by both. Each party warrants no conflict/encumbrance on contributed work.
- **Get an entertainment/comics attorney to review before signature** — expected $1-2k, worth it.

#### The 3 things that are non-negotiable (JD's floors)
1. **Net Revenue definition + quarterly statements + audit right.** No accounting ambiguity, ever.
2. **"Names are changed" compliance applies to the art.** No recognizable real-likeness in the 15/Rick Steves/EMA figures.
3. **V2/V3 as options, and JD owns story/characters.** The trilogy can survive any single artist's exit.

#### The 2 things to give the artist without a fight
1. **Physical originals + portfolio + convention sales.** It's standard, it's free goodwill, and it makes the pages worth collecting.
2. **45% net-revenue share over 40%.** The difference between $40k and $45k over 3 volumes is a rounding error today and a loyalty signal for a 4-6 year partnership. [Estimates]
