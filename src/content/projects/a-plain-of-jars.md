## A Plain of Jars — The Memoir

I am looking at a sprawling body of work that spans years and mediums. It is not just a memoir; it’s an intricate web of stories, numbers, and research designed to tell one cohesive tale: my life from childhood through recovery.

### What Is This For?
The goal here is simple yet profound: to document my journey in a way that provides both personal insight and practical guidance for others facing similar challenges. By breaking down each part into its component pieces—be they creative processes or financial models—I hope to offer clarity on how things are built without becoming lost in the minutiae of day-to-day operations.

## The Structure
The project bible lays out all aspects of A Plain of Jars (projects/a-plain-of-jars-bible). It serves as a roadmap, detailing everything from character arcs to plot points and even potential marketing strategies. Each piece within this structure has been meticulously planned with an eye towards coherence and impact.

### Research Addendum
A significant portion of my work involves research, particularly around themes like recovery and personal growth. This is where I introduce 'That wasn't Claude Code...the free open source model' (projects/that-wasn-t-claude-code-it-was-opencode-zen-the-free) which adds depth by connecting the narrative threads through external projects such as The MLM Truth Project (projects/a-plain-of-jars-story-bible-addendum-the-mlm-truth-project).

## Financial Transparency
Understanding costs and valuations are integral to this project’s success. Posts like ‘Kickstarter Financial Model’ (projects/kickstarter-financial-model) provide a clear breakdown of expected expenses, revenue streams, and potential outcomes for A Plain of Jars. This transparency ensures that there is no room for misinterpretation or hidden agendas.

### Artist Partnerships & Term Sheets
Collaborations with artists play an essential role in bringing these stories to life visually. An example can be seen in the draft term sheet outlining partnership terms between myself and contributing artists (projects/artist-partnership-term-sheet). Such agreements ensure mutual respect and understanding while fostering creative synergy.

## Serialization Strategy
The serialization strategy leverages platforms like Substack as part of The Light Field series (projects/the-light-field-substack-serialization-strategy) where serialized chapters are released alongside ongoing research updates, keeping readers engaged over time rather than overwhelming them all at once.

### Content Channels
Another key aspect is leveraging multiple content channels for maximum reach—such as a dedicated YouTube channel focused on behind-the-scenes insights into A Plain of Jars’ creation process (projects/youtube-process-channel)—and cross-promotion through related projects such as Propagandhi Album Analysis (projects/i-need-help-developing-a-youtube-channel-from-scratch).

## Current State and Next Steps
Currently, the project bible provides solid groundwork with additional addenda enriching specific thematic areas. Financial models offer transparency around costs and potential returns while term sheets ensure fair artist collaborations. Serialization strategies keep readers engaged over time rather than overwhelming them upfront.

The next steps involve refining these components further based on feedback received from early readers or contributors involved in various stages of development.
