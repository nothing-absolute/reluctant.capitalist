## athf character designs

## CHARACTER DESIGNS: "SMONER BOBERST" (ATHF STYLE)
**Art Style Reference:** *Aqua Teen Hunger Force*
**Aesthetic:** Low-fi Flash animation, intentionally crude, bold black outlines, flat solid colors, characters are anthropomorphic objects with floating hands (no arms), minimal animation, Adult Swim energy.

---

## THE CORE CONCEPT

Just like Frylock, Shake, and Meatwad are literally fast food items walking around a suburban New Jersey house, every character in "Smoner Boberst" is an anthropomorphic **object related to their role in the story**. They have simple faces, floating mitten-hands, and exist in a world where nobody acknowledges how weird this is.

---

## 1. SMONER BOBERST
**Object:** A giant guitar pick.
**Description:** Smoner is a large, worn-out, triangular guitar pick — the kind you'd find wedged in a couch cushion. He's scuffed, chipped at the edges, and perpetually sweating (visible droplet animations). He has droopy, half-lidded eyes near the top, a frowning mouth, and greasy yarn-like hair hanging off one side. He wears a tiny leather jacket that only covers the top third of his pick-body. Floating mitten-hands.

**Personality Note:** Talks in a monotone mumble until he suddenly snaps into manic infomercial energy mid-sentence. Like Master Shake's unearned confidence meets Meatwad's sadness.

**Color Palette:** Faded olive green body, black jacket, gray sweat drops.

---

## 2. THE NARRATOR
**Object:** A corkboard.
**Description:** The Narrator is literally a walking, talking conspiracy corkboard. He is a large, flat, rectangular cork panel with legs. His surface is covered in pinned photos, newspaper clippings, and red string — but the red string also trails behind him wherever he goes like a tail, getting tangled on furniture. He has two giant googly eyes (the craft-store kind, rattling when he moves) pinned to his upper section, and a mouth cut into the cork. Push pins stick out of him at random angles.

**Personality Note:** He talks at 2x speed, constantly interrupting himself. He's the Shake of the group — loud, wrong, and absolutely convinced he's the smartest person in the room.

**Color Palette:** Cork brown body, red string, white/yellow paper clippings, silver push pins.

---

## 3. BUSTER (THE DOG)
**Object:** A dog bone.
**Description:** Buster is an oversized, anthropomorphic Milk-Bone dog biscuit. He has a simple, content smile, half-closed sleepy eyes, and wears a backwards baseball cap. He's the Meatwad of the group — sweet, dumb, along for the ride. He just sort of exists in the room, occasionally saying something accidentally profound. Floating mitten-hands. Short stubby legs.

**Personality Note:** Never reacts to anything with urgency. The Narrator is screaming about the Illuminati and Buster just goes "...cool."

**Color Palette:** Tan/biscuit brown body, blue baseball cap.

---

## 4. THE NEWS ANCHOR ("BARNABY TUSK")
**Object:** An old CRT television set.
**Description:** The News Anchor is a bulky, wood-paneled 1970s CRT television wearing a maroon suit jacket, paisley tie, and pocket square. His face IS the screen — a mustachioed, somber anchorman face is permanently displayed on it, occasionally flickering with static when he gets emotional. Antenna ears stick up from the top. He has the gravitas of a walrus and the reliability of a TV that needs to be smacked to work.

**Personality Note:** Delivers horrific news with absolute sincerity and zero self-awareness, like a local news anchor who doesn't realize how insane everything he's saying sounds.

**Color Palette:** Wood-grain brown body, maroon jacket, blue-gray screen glow.

---

## 5. THE SHARKS (INVESTOR PANEL)
**Objects:** Various expensive household items.
**Description:** Each "Shark" is a different luxury object sitting in oversized leather chairs:

| Shark | Object | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mr. Wonderful** | A diamond ring | Enormous, gaudy, sparkles constantly. Cruel little eyes set in the band. |
| **Mark Cuban** | A stack of hundred dollar bills | Held together by a rubber band. One eye is a holographic security strip. |
| **Shark 3** | A gold Rolex watch | Ticking audibly at all times. Impatient. The ticking gets faster when angry. |
| **Shark 4** | A yacht (miniature) | Literally a small boat sitting in a chair. This is the one whose yacht gets blown up. Has a tiny captain's hat. |

**Personality Note:** They all talk over each other and say "I'm out" reflexively.

---

## 6. THE "EMO" DEVICE
**Object:** Itself.
**Description:** The EMO device is a small, featureless black rectangle (like a TV remote with no buttons). It never does anything on screen. It might not even be real. Smoner holds it up reverently and everyone just stares at it. It has one tiny red LED that blinks.

---

## SETTING NOTES (ATHF STYLE)

The backgrounds should mirror ATHF's suburban mundanity:
- **The Narrator's House:** A rundown New Jersey ranch house. The interior is Carl's house meets the Aqua Teens' living room — wood paneling, stained carpet, a busted recliner, and the conspiracy board taking up an entire wall.
- **The Shark Tank Set:** Intentionally cheap-looking. The "set" is clearly just a conference room with a banner taped to the wall. The lighting rig that falls is visibly held up by duct tape.
- **The News Desk:** A single desk in front of a green screen that's slightly the wrong shade of green.

INT. MESSY APARTMENT - DAY

A cramped room. Walls obscured by a massive corkboard covered in newspaper clippings, low-res printed photos, and a chaotic web of red string.

NARRATOR
Wait... it was screamo that sucked... no, ska... punk... blink-182... 

He grabs a tangled ball of cables connecting three Mac Studios stacked on a milk crate.

NARRATOR
So me and Buster, we built a hack. We’re using the compute of all the tech executives’ Mac Studios to run billions of Earth simulations to see how humanity truly evolved. Is it evolution, or was the Bible right this whole time?

You’re sweating on my carpet. This is a joke to you?

SMONER

No! I want you to like me! EMO AI was just a foot in the door. It is the scene! A project from Peter Thiel's secret society, backed by the Democratic Party...

What are you talking about?

SMONER
Quick poll. Do you own multiple boats?

SMONER
Think of your favorite big one. The taxes you wrote off as a depreciable asset. Now open your eyes.

Breaking news. Smoner Boberst has reportedly shot himself.

ANCHOR (V.O.)
Back and to the left, folks.
