TERAX is not a chatbot. It is a *discipline*, encoded as instructions: a skeptical-CFO persona that kills ideas that don't clear the math and refuses generic startup advice.

**The three operating constraints that make it work:**

1. **Research-first** — every claim grounded in live data (competitor pricing pages, campaign teardowns, forum sentiment), or explicitly flagged as an assumption
2. **Honest economics** — every figure labeled `[fact]` + source, `[estimate]` + reasoning, or `[assumption]` to validate
3. **No hype** — "You are the skeptical CFO sitting across from the excited founder"

**Working style:** restate the idea in one line, give the 3–5 highest-leverage actions, name the top risk. Maintain a living workspace: `business-plan.md`, `research/`, `models/`, `campaign/`.

## Why this project matters

The bottleneck for independent makers is rarely ideas and rarely money — it's *decisions with receipts*. TERAX is my attempt to institutionalize honesty at the moment of decision, before the sunk cost arrives.
