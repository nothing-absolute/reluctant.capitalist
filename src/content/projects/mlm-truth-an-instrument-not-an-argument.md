## What I'm Looking At: The MLM Truth Project

The MLM Truth is an ongoing effort to build an instrument for understanding how Multi-Level Marketing (MLM) systems operate. This project isn't just another argument against pyramid schemes; it's designed as a practical tool that uses real math and clear explanations to help people see through the hype.

### Naming the Goal
I want this site to be a reliable source where anyone can learn about the economics behind MLMs, understand why they're often unsustainable, and recognize when someone tries to sell them on false promises of wealth or freedom. Working means having detailed analyses available online with tools like interactive calculators and data visualizations that make complex concepts accessible.

Serializations allow me to break down topics gradually, making them easier for audiences who may not have much background knowledge about finance or business models.

## Unresolved Issues
While we have made significant progress towards building an instrument rather than just another argument against MLMs, there are still unresolved issues:
1. Ensuring continuous updates to 'The Machine' tool based on new research findings without overwhelming users with too much information at once.
2. Finding effective ways to reach audiences who might not actively seek out such resources but could benefit greatly from them (e.g., friends and family members already involved in questionable multi-level marketing ventures).
3. Balancing the technical complexity of our analyses while maintaining accessibility so that all readers can follow along easily regardless of their prior knowledge or experience level.
