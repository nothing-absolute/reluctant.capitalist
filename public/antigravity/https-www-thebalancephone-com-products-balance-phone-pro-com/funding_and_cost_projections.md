## 3. Initial Hardware Cost Breakdown (Off-the-Shelf Strategy)

To maintain healthy margins and guarantee delivery, initial tiers use existing hardware platforms flashed with the secure Lumina OS.

### A. Lumina Pro (Standard Hardware Edition)
*   **Target Retail Price:** $399.00
*   **Hardware Baseline:** Mid-range Android smartphone (OLED/LCD display, modern processor).
*   **Target COGS (Wholesale + Flashing):** $230.00 (Gross Margin: 42.3%)

| Component | Cost | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wholesale Device Cost** | $180.00 | Bulk purchase of a solid mid-range device (e.g., standard Samsung Galaxy A-series or similar). |
| **Licensing & Flashing Labor** | $25.00 | Labor for flashing custom Lumina OS and locking bootloader. |
| **Packaging & Accessories**| $10.00 | Custom Lumina-branded eco-box and accessories. |
| **Fulfillment & Buffer** | $15.00 | Logistics and margin of error padding. |
| **Total Cost per Unit** | **$230.00** | |

### B. Lumina Kids (Rugged Edition)
*   **Target Retail Price:** $249.00
*   **Hardware Baseline:** Off-the-shelf rugged Android phone (e.g., Ulefone Armor).
