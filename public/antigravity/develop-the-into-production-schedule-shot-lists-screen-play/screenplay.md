# "SMONER BOBERST"
**A Mock Documentary Short**
Inspired by *Nirvana the Band the Show*

---

**[SCENE START]**

**INT. MESSY APARTMENT - DAY**

A cramped, dimly lit room. The walls are mostly obscured by a massive corkboard covered in newspaper clippings, low-res printed photos, and a chaotic web of red string. 

Handheld camera, shaking slightly. 

THE NARRATOR (30s, frantic, unkempt, bags under his eyes) stands in front of the board. A Golden Retriever sits obediently beside him, looking completely unfazed.

NARRATOR
(Talking fast, manic)
I’m from a town three and a half hours outside of Mahaoma, New Jersey. Same age as Smoner. I grew up on those Lite Bright records. I drove to the shows. It made my life worth living in high school. It made me believe I could make music. 

He points to a blurry photo of a young indie rocker.

NARRATOR (CONT'D)
And now he's on Shark Tank? Selling out? I know this can’t be real. The Shark Tank producers must be blackmailing him. Or kidnapping. Or it's MKUltra. Illuminati! Wait... weren't those old shows held at the Odd Fellows Hall? 

He traces a red string from the photo to a crude drawing of an eye.

NARRATOR (CONT'D)
Wait... it was screamo that sucked... no, ska... punk... blink-182... none of it makes sense anymore! 

He grabs a massive, tangled ball of cables connecting three Mac Studios stacked haphazardly on a milk crate.

NARRATOR (CONT'D)
So me and Buster—
(He gestures to the dog)
—we built a hack. We’re using the compute of all the tech executives’ Mac Studios to run billions of Earth simulations to see how humanity *truly* evolved. Is it evolution, or was the Bible right this whole time? We’re close. We are *so* close.

**SMASH CUT TO:**

**INT. TELEVISION SCREEN - DAY**

A painfully cheesy, 1990s CGI graphic spins onto the screen with garish neon colors. The text reads: **NOW That's What I Call Capitalism!**

VOICE OVER (V.O.)
(Stumbling, awkward)
NOW That's What I Call... Cap—capita—capitalism! 

**INT. LIVING ROOM - DAY**

The camera is pointing at a TV screen, someone is frantically flicking through channels. Static. A soap opera. A cooking show. 

*SHARK TANK STING plays.*

**INT. SHARK TANK SET - DAY**

Big, sweeping crane shot over the Sharks. They look impatient. 

Standing on the carpet is SMONER BOBERST (30s, leather jacket, vintage band tee, sweating profusely). He clutches a small, black, rectangular device. 

MR. WONDERFUL
(Off-screen)
You’re sweating on my carpet, Smoner. This is a joke to you?

SMONER
(Voice cracking, slipping into an infomercial cadence)
No, Mr. Wonderful! I want you to like me! This isn't just a device. EMO AI was just a foot in the door. It is the scene! A project from Peter Thiel's secret society, backed by the Democratic Party...

MARK CUBAN
(Interrupting)
What are you talking about?

Smoner snaps out of the panic. He takes a deep breath. His demeanor shifts. Dead serious.

SMONER
Quick poll. Do you own multiple boats? 

The Sharks mumble confused "yes"s. 

SMONER
Think of your favorite big one. The taxes you wrote off as a depreciable asset. Now open your eyes. 
(Beat)
Wait, did I say close? Shit, fuck, cunt. Oh, fucking rimjobs on cock, my bad. Shake it off. 

Smoner clears his throat and points to a monitor behind them. 

SMONER
Look in your monitor. That’s the boat you were thinking of.

**OVERSHOULDER SHOT:**

We look past Smoner's shoulder to the monitor. It looks like a VHS camcorder feed from a drone. An aerial shot of a massive luxury megayacht. A red targeting reticle blinks over it.

SMONER
What if I put a bomb on there? And I'll blow it up right now unless you end all data centers and capitalism itself. 

The Sharks laugh nervously. 

SMONER
(Screaming)
BOOM!

**INSERT:** 
Stock footage of a massive explosion. (Repeat explosion effect for 5 different yachts).

**INT. SHARK TANK SET - CONTINUOUS**

The Sharks are horrified. 

Smoner smiles, holding up the black rectangular device.

SMONER
The flip: What if I told you I created a device that could have prevented that, and it's only three hundred dollars? No subscription. Free.99 for life.

Suddenly, a loud CRASH off-camera. The lighting rig falls. The camera violently tilts sideways. Chaos ensues. Yelling. 

**CUT TO:**

**INT. NEWS DESK - LATER**

A Local News Anchor, styled like Ron Burgundy, looks somberly into the camera.

ANCHOR
Breaking news tonight. Indie rock darling Conor Oberst—sorry, Smoner Boberst—has reportedly shot himself. 

**INSERT FOOTAGE:** 
Obviously AI-generated, glitchy footage of Smoner aimlessly firing a gun in the air before turning it on himself. 

ANCHOR (V.O.)
Back and to the left, folks. Back and to the left. 

**INT. NEWS DESK - CONTINUOUS**

ANCHOR
We have received a statement that his body was cremated immediately. Due to the lighting and camera rigging falling on him. And, allegedly, some bystanders kicked him like a dead horse just to be sure he wasn't a demon. Reports indicate the body was just... goo. A biohazard. Oh yeah, and he shot himself. Have a great night, Mahaoma.

**[SCENE END]**
