# Pre-Production Plan: "Smoner Boberst" Mock Doc

## 1. Production Schedule
**Total Shoot Days:** 2 Days (Weekend Shoot)
**Crew Size:** Skeleton crew (Director/DP, Audio, Gaffer, PA/Art Director)

### Day 1: The Conspiracy & The News
* **Location:** Messy Apartment / News Desk Setup (can be same location, different rooms or green screen)
* **08:00 AM - 09:00 AM:** Load in and set dress "Conspiracy Board" (Art Dept heavy).
* **09:00 AM - 12:00 PM:** Shoot Narrator scenes (Handheld, lots of takes for manic energy). Include dog wrangling.
* **12:00 PM - 01:00 PM:** LUNCH
* **01:00 PM - 03:00 PM:** Shoot "TV watcher" inserts and channel surfing elements.
* **03:00 PM - 06:00 PM:** Set up and shoot the Ron Burgundy News Anchor desk scene.

### Day 2: The Shark Tank Pitch
* **Location:** Studio or rented boardroom space dressed as a TV set.
* **08:00 AM - 10:00 AM:** Lighting setup for "Shark Tank" look (multiple angles, dramatic overheads). 
* **10:00 AM - 12:30 PM:** Smoner's pitch coverage (wides, close-ups on Smoner sweating).
* **12:30 PM - 01:30 PM:** LUNCH
* **01:30 PM - 04:30 PM:** Shoot "Sharks" reactions (can use stand-ins or actors mimicking the Sharks).
* **04:30 PM - 06:00 PM:** The "Ruckus" – safely dropping a small prop lighting rig, shaking the camera, capturing the chaotic ending.

## 2. Shot List

### Scene 1: The Conspiracy (Apartment)
1. **Wide Shot (Handheld):** Establishing the room, the Narrator, the dog, and the massive corkboard.
2. **Close-Up (Handheld):** Narrator's manic face as he speaks.
3. **Insert (Macro):** A blurry photo of Smoner Boberst on the board.
4. **Insert (Macro):** Red string connecting the photo to an eye drawing.
5. **Medium Shot:** Narrator picking up the tangled cables connected to the Mac Studios.

### Scene 2: Commercial Interruption
6. **Screen Capture/VFX:** Full screen 90s CGI "NOW That's What I Call Capitalism".
7. **POV Shot:** Watching the TV, flicking through channels.

### Scene 3: The Pitch (Shark Tank)
8. **Establishing Crane/Jib Shot:** Sweeping over the Sharks' chairs towards Smoner.
9. **Medium Close-Up (Smoner):** Sweating, looking nervous. Sitcom/Studio lighting.
10. **Close-Up (Smoner):** Stumbling over his words ("shit, fuck, cunt").
11. **Over-The-Shoulder (Smoner's POV):** Looking at the Sharks.
12. **Reverse Over-The-Shoulder:** Looking past Smoner at the monitor showing the drone footage.
13. **Insert (Monitor):** Megayacht drone feed with red reticle.
14. **Close-Up (Sharks):** Horrified reactions.
15. **Medium Shot (Smoner):** Holding up the "EMO" device, smiling. "The flip."
16. **Dutch Angle/Chaos:** Camera violently shakes, tilts sideways. A lighting rig (prop) falls into frame.

### Scene 4: The News (News Desk)
17. **Medium Shot (Static):** News anchor looking into camera.
18. **Insert/VFX:** AI-generated footage of Smoner shooting himself (stylized/glitchy).

## 3. Assets & Props Needed

**Art Department / Props:**
* **Conspiracy Board:** Large corkboard, push pins, red yarn, printed low-res photos, newspaper clippings, hand-drawn illuminati symbols.
* **The "EMO" Device:** A sleek, black, rectangular block (could just be a painted hard drive or block of wood).
* **Mac Studio Rig:** 3x Mac Studios (or empty chassis/boxes dressed to look like them), milk crate, massive ball of tangled ethernet/power cables.
* **Smoner's Wardrobe:** Vintage band t-shirt (Lite Bright or similar), distressed leather jacket, jeans. (Must look sweaty - spray bottle with water/glycerin).
* **News Anchor Wardrobe:** 70s style maroon suit, fake mustache (Ron Burgundy style).

**Live Elements:**
* **Buster the Dog:** A Golden Retriever (needs to sit and stay).

---

## 4. Visual & Post-Production Needs (VFX)

1. **"NOW That's What I Call Capitalism" Graphic:**
   * Needs to be created in Blender or After Effects using early-90s CGI aesthetics (garish colors, simple 3D text, low framerate).
2. **Megayacht Drone Feed:**
   * Stock footage of a luxury yacht.
   * Add VHS distortion overlays, timecodes, and a red tracking reticle.
3. **Yacht Explosions:**
   * Stock explosion VFX composited over the yacht footage. Needs to look jarring and sudden.
4. **AI Suicide Footage:**
   * Use an AI video generator (like Runway or Pika) to generate glitchy, dream-like footage of the character with a gun. The goal is for it to look *obviously* AI-generated and slightly grotesque.
