## 4. SCREENPLAY (FOUNTAIN / SCRIPT FORMAT)

```text
[SCENE START]

INT. MESSY APARTMENT - DAY

A cramped room. Walls obscured by a massive corkboard covered in newspaper clippings, low-res printed photos, and a chaotic web of red string. 

Handheld camera, shaking slightly. 

THE NARRATOR (30s, frantic) stands in front of the board. A Golden Retriever (BUSTER) sits beside him, unfazed.

NARRATOR
(Talking fast)
I’m from a town three and a half hours outside of Mahaoma. Same age as Smoner. Grew up on those Lite Bright records. Drove to the shows. It made my life worth living in high school. Made me believe I could make music. 

He points to a blurry photo of a young indie rocker.

NARRATOR
And now he's on Shark Tank? Selling out? This can’t be real. Producers must be blackmailing him. Or kidnapping. MKUltra. Illuminati! Wait... weren't those old shows held at the Odd Fellows Hall? 

He traces a red string from the photo to an eye drawing.

NARRATOR
Wait... it was screamo that sucked... no, ska... punk... blink-182... 

He grabs a tangled ball of cables connecting three Mac Studios stacked on a milk crate.

NARRATOR
So me and Buster, we built a hack. We’re using the compute of all the tech executives’ Mac Studios to run billions of Earth simulations to see how humanity truly evolved. Is it evolution, or was the Bible right this whole time? 

SMASH CUT TO:

INT. TELEVISION SCREEN - DAY

A cheesy, 1990s CGI graphic spins onto screen. Text: NOW That's What I Call Capitalism!

VOICE OVER (V.O.)
(Stumbling)
NOW That's What I Call... Cap—capita—capitalism! 

INT. SHARK TANK SET - DAY

Standing on the carpet is SMONER BOBERST (30s, leather jacket, vintage band tee, sweating). He clutches a small, black device. 

MR. WONDERFUL (O.S.)
You’re sweating on my carpet. This is a joke to you?

SMONER
(Infomercial cadence)
No! I want you to like me! EMO AI was just a foot in the door. It is the scene! A project from Peter Thiel's secret society, backed by the Democratic Party...

MARK CUBAN
What are you talking about?

Smoner snaps out of the panic. Dead serious.

SMONER
Quick poll. Do you own multiple boats? 

The Sharks mumble confused "yes"s. 

SMONER
Think of your favorite big one. The taxes you wrote off as a depreciable asset. Now open your eyes. 
(Beat)
Wait, did I say close? Shit, fuck, cunt. Oh, fucking rimjobs on cock, my bad. Shake it off. 

Smoner points to a monitor behind them. 

SMONER
Look in your monitor. That’s the boat you were thinking of.

OVERSHOULDER SHOT: The monitor shows a VHS drone feed of a massive luxury megayacht. A red reticle blinks over it.

SMONER
What if I put a bomb on there? I'll blow it up right now unless you end all data centers and capitalism itself. BOOM!

INSERT: Stock footage of yacht explosions.

INT. SHARK TANK SET - CONTINUOUS

The Sharks are horrified. Smoner smiles, holding up the device.

SMONER
The flip: What if I told you I created a device that could have prevented that, and it's only three hundred dollars? No subscription. Free.99 for life.

A loud CRASH. The lighting rig falls. The camera violently tilts sideways. Yelling. 

CUT TO:

INT. NEWS DESK - LATER

A Local News Anchor (Ron Burgundy style) looks somberly into the camera.

ANCHOR
Breaking news. Smoner Boberst has reportedly shot himself. 

INSERT: AI-generated, glitchy footage of Smoner firing a gun in the air before turning it on himself. 

ANCHOR (V.O.)
Back and to the left, folks. 

INT. NEWS DESK - CONTINUOUS

ANCHOR
We received a statement his body was cremated immediately. Due to the lighting and camera rigging falling on him. And, allegedly, bystanders kicked him like a dead horse to be sure he wasn't a demon. Body was just goo. A biohazard. Oh yeah, and he shot himself. Have a great night.

[SCENE END]
```
