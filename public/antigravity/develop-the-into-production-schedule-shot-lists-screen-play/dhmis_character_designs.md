# CHARACTER DESIGNS: "SMONER BOBERST" (DHMIS STYLE)
**Art Style Reference:** *Don't Hug Me I'm Scared*
**Aesthetic:** Creepy felt puppets, practical effects, unsettling "kid's TV show" vibes, uncanny valley, liminal spaces.

---

## 1. SMONER BOBERST
**Material:** Distressed felt and stringy yarn.
**Description:** Smoner is transformed into a deeply sad, sweaty muppet. He has heavy, drooping button eyes with dark felt bags under them. His hair is made of greasy-looking gray yarn, and he wears a miniature, perfectly tailored (but grimy) leather jacket. He looks like a depressed indie rocker trapped in a children's educational program.

## 2. THE NARRATOR
**Material:** Patchwork fabric, googly eyes, red yarn.
**Description:** The Narrator is a frantic, paranoid puppet assembled from mismatched scraps of fabric, resembling a creature that was made in a hurry. He has excessively large googly eyes that stare unblinkingly. Instead of red string on his corkboard, the red yarn seems to be unraveling from his own puppet body, connecting him physically to his conspiracy theories.

## 3. BUSTER (THE DOG)
**Material:** Cheap mascot costume fur.
**Description:** Instead of a cute puppet, Buster is portrayed as someone wearing a very cheap, slightly dirty, and unsettlingly realistic Golden Retriever mascot head over regular human clothes (jeans and a t-shirt). He sits completely still on the couch, creating a deeply liminal and creepy presence in the background of the Narrator's chaotic room.

## 4. THE SHARKS
**Material:** Carved upholstery foam and felt.
**Description:** The wealthy investors are massive, grotesque, brightly colored shark puppets. They are carved from thick foam with sharp, jagged felt teeth and dead, shiny black button eyes. They wear oversized, expensive-looking human business suits, creating a terrifying contrast between predatory sea creatures and corporate executives.
