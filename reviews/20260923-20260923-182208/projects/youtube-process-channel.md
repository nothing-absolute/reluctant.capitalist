## YouTube Process Channel — "The Making of A Plain of Jars"

*Strategy capture 2026-09-19. JD confirmed: THIS is the go project — a YouTube channel documenting the creation of the GN, whose content also feeds revenue streams *outside* the book project. The "movie in a movie about a movie" vibe. Numbers are [estimates].*

### Positioning (the POV that makes it work)
The channel IS the meta-artifact: **"I'm making the graphic novel of my twenties' indie scene — filmed while it happens."** The book is the movie; making the book is the movie-about-the-movie; the channel is the movie-about-the-movie-about-the-movie. This is what converts a craft channel into a documentary with a serial arc.

- Production timeline = release calendar. 9-14 months (hybrid) or 18-24 (hand-drawn) of real work = 40-120 episodes of free b-roll. Zero marginal content cost.
- Seasoned arc: Season 1 = V1. Each volume = a season.

### Format buckets (weekly mix)
1. **Process/craft** (drawing reviews, AI pipeline builds, world-building from the photo boxes) — the work itself on camera.
2. **Story/context docusodes** (the indie scene, the reunion wave, "who are the 15?" teasers — great trailer material).
3. **Milestone reveals** (artist on-boarding, KS prep, print day, unboxing).
4. **Shorts** (60s repurposes of all above; KS CTA posts).

Cadence: 1-2 long-form (10-25 min) + 2-3 Shorts/wk.

### Revenue streams (incl. "outside the project, using its content")
1. **YPP ads** — niche craft/doc long-form RPM ~$3-6/1k views [est]. Need 1k subs + 4k watch-hrs to enter.
2. **Memberships** ($1.99-4.99/mo) — superfan tier, process-room access.
3. **Sponsorships** — the tool-maker market is real: drawing tablets, Clip Studio/Procreate, GPUs/AI render platforms, drawing supplies. Mid-size (50-200k) channels book $500-3k/video [est].
4. **Affiliate** — Amazon art-kit lists, software subs.
5. **KS + newsletter funnel** — its REAL year-1 job: backer acquisition (campaign's biggest marketing line) at ~$0 marginal.
6. **Later: the process content compiles into the companion-doc IP** — the channel's archive is the doc pitch for the "Meet Me in the Bathroom"-adjacent adaptation tail.

This is where the actual income of the whole project lives — not the KS surplus, not the speculative tail. The channel funds the making; the book funds the campaign; each feeds the other.

### Compliance (channel-specific)
- **On-camera consent:** filiming real people (the 15, band members) needs consent or no identifiable faces. "Names are changed" protects the TEXT; video consent is a different bar.
- **No doxxing, no spoilers:** never reveal who the 15 are; teasers yes, plot drops no.
- **AI disclosure:** YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure (Mar 2025) + backer/publisher honesty — "AI-assisted backgrounds/cleanup; story & figure work human-drawn" in every relevant video.
- **Own-model ownership note:** see the LLM-ownership question below — output copyright still rests on human authorship, our model or not.

### Kill criteria / honest guardrails
- Don't half-bake: a diary-channel with no POV is noise; 40 episodes of nothing kills the funnel. The docusode POV is the differentiator.
- KPI at month 6: 1k subs OR 50k views/mo OR 200+ newsletter signups via channel. If none, revise format before burning year 2.
