**09:31 - Kickstarter choice: Sentinel (boat monitor) over OpenDoor (doorbell)**
Decision rationale for which project to launch first on Kickstarter → **Sentinel wins as first campaign**:
- Fulfillment risk: Sentinel is ESP32+LTE-M+sensors (~10x simpler to ship); OpenDoor needs camera, solar, 2-way audio, battery-life claims, and a full iOS/Android app.
- Category crowding: video doorbells are one of the most failed categories on Kickstarter; boat monitors have a thin field.
- Attackable incumbent: Siren Marine $600+$25/mo vs Sentinel $149 one-time; OpenDoor's Ring sells at $99, weak price contrast.
- Margins: Sentinel ~$44 BOM @ $149 ≈ 77% margin + $279 dual-pack; OpenDoor $89–129 thin.
- Buyer intent: boat owners (fear-driven, $50k+ asset) convert high; doorbell buyers price-compare.
- Trade-off: OpenDoor has bigger ceiling/TAM and cleaner "$0/mo" story → save for campaign #2.
- Open risk on Sentinel: "no subscription" needs honest wording (BYO $2/mo SIM or marina WiFi, not free cellular). Demand validation docs (competitor-review-mining, search-volume) still unfilled — do before launch.

**11:20 - Sentinel FCC certification: mandatory, $12k–$25k, co-location is the catch**
Researched the FCC path for Sentinel (ESP32-S3 WiFi/BT + SIM7000G/SIM7080G LTE-M). Wrote `Prototypes/sentinel/plan/certification.md` and corrected two wrong claims in the plan.
- **Cert is required** — Sentinel is an *intentional radiator*; §2.803 (marketing) + §2.1204 (import, customs checks FCC IDs) mean no legal US sale/fulfillment without equipment authorization. Old plan's "ship under Part 15 self-declaration while formal cert runs" is wrong — SDoC covers only the host's Part 15B digital emissions, never the transmitters.
- **Modules already cover the radios**: ESP32-WROOM-32E (`2AC7Z-ESP32WROOM32E`), SIM7000G (`2AJYU-SIM7000G`, single modular, mobile host ≥20cm, antenna gain limits).
- **The catch = co-location.** SIM7000G grant: "must not be co-located... except in accordance with FCC multi-transmitter evaluation procedures." WiFi + LTE run simultaneously → triggers RF exposure eval (KDB 447498, MPE ok — boat mount is >20cm) + co-location emissions (KDB 996369 D04, needs CMW500 call box = expensive line).
- **Realistic cost/time: $12k–$25k US, 8–12 wks** (vs $8k in the plan — updated `business-plan.md` funds table $8k→$15k, working capital $6.5k→$4.5k). IC Canada ~$2k, CE/RED+RoHS EU ~$8–12k (Year 2).
- **Labeling (KDB 784748):** "Contains FCC ID: 2AJYU-SIM7000G" + "2AC7Z-ESP32WROOM32E" + Part 15 statement.
- Adjacent: BYO SIM rides on module carrier certs (no separate carrier cert); UN38.3 battery shipping cert; optional FCC Cyber Trust Mark (2025) = campaign differentiator.
- Action: get lab quotes Month 1, lock module FCC IDs, design in ≥20cm antenna separation.
