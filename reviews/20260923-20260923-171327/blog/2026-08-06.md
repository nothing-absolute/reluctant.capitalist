**JASMINE Worst-Case: Minimum Events to Break Even (2026-08-06)**
- Doc: `Prototypes/jasmine-bus-rental/03-worst-case.md`. Rule: event contribution = revenue − its own costs (driver/fuel/fees/cleaning/site); break even = cumulative contribution ≥ $11,500 fixed. Driver already paid inside each event.

- Key: the break-even floor is stable at 2 events in base AND worst case because SB+F1 dominate; worst case eats profit, not the floor. Super Bowl alone ≈ entire fixed-cost bill.
- Decision rules: (1) SB+F1 are the whole break-even — pre-sell 6-9 mo out w/ deposits; (2) in a bad year shrink the route, never drop the driver (break-even driver rate $406/day); (3) failure point is occupancy not driver pay (committed driver = same cost empty or full); (4) never run a stop you wouldn't run at 75% occupancy.
