### Core Idea
Build an **Earthship home** by taking advantage of **incentives to build a home in struggling states**. Find as many opportunities as possible across the **US and abroad**, and design an Earthship adapted to **each location's weather and available local materials**.

### The Angle
- Struggling states offer incentives (land, tax breaks, grants, revitalization programs) for building homes — leverage them.
- Scan for incentive programs across the US, then expand abroad where conditions fit.
- Each build is a **localized design**: Earthship principles adapted to that climate (thermal mass, water, power) and to locally available materials — no two builds are identical.
- **Partner with the Earthship Academy** and other Earthship builders in the US and abroad for methodology, training, and credibility.

### Why It Matters
- Earthships = self-sufficient, off-grid, largely free-to-maintain homes built from waste/earth materials.
- Home ownership in struggling areas can transform communities — and demonstrates the alternative (non-capitalist) way of building, aligning with the big-picture mission.

### Research Areas
- State/local incentive programs for homebuilding in struggling regions (land grants, homestead programs, tax abatements, rural development).
- Earthship Academy + established builders (US and international) — partnership/credentialing.
- Climate-specific design variants (cold, hot-arid, humid, heavy-precip) and their material palettes.
- Local material availability per candidate location (tires, glass, earth, timber, straw, etc.).
