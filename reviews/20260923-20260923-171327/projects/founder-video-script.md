## OpenPlotter — Founder Video Script
#### "No VC" Pitch — Shot Underway, Raw

**Total runtime target:** ~2:15–2:45
**Format:** Single continuous take preferred over cutaways. If cuts are needed for length, cut on hard sentence breaks only — no jump-cut "vlogger" pacing, no music swells under the voice.
**Location:** At the helm or cockpit, boat underway at low idle / no-wake speed.
**Camera:** Handheld or gimbal, medium-close. No lav mic hidden — visible mic is fine.
**Do not:** add a title card intro, do not add background music under dialogue, do not cut to b-roll of the product during the talking sections — save product shots for the tier section of the page, not this video.

#### [0:00–0:15] Cold open — no slate, no logo, just the founder mid-thought

**VISUAL:** Camera already rolling as if it caught the founder starting to talk. Boat wake visible behind them. No graphic overlay yet.

**FOUNDER (on camera):**
> "I'm not going to do the thing where I stand in front of a whiteboard and tell you we're disrupting an industry. I'm standing on my boat, because that's where this problem actually lives."

#### [0:15–0:45] The problem, stated plainly

**VISUAL:** Founder gestures naturally toward the helm/dash if visible in frame — no zoom-in on a logo, keep it loose.

**FOUNDER:**
> "Marine electronics companies charge you a subscription to look at charts the government already gives away for free. They sell you a nine-hundred-dollar screen that's built out of parts that cost a fraction of that. And if it breaks after the warranty runs out, you're done — because you don't have the schematics, they do."
>
> "That's not a technology problem. That's a business model built on you not having options."

#### [0:45–1:15] The turn — what we built instead

**VISUAL:** No cutaway to product yet — hold on the founder. Let the confidence of the claim carry the shot.

**FOUNDER:**
> "So we built a chartplotter on a Raspberry Pi. It reads the same NOAA charts, for free, forever. It picks up AIS off a thirty-four dollar radio dongle. And every schematic, every board file, every line of firmware is already public on GitHub — right now, not after this campaign hits some goal."
>
> "You can go check that today. I'll wait."

*(Brief pause — don't cut this, let it sit for a beat. It's a dare, and it should feel like one.)*

#### [1:15–1:45] The pledge — this is the emotional peak of the video

**VISUAL:** Tighter framing here if possible (subtle push-in, not a hard zoom). This is the one moment where slightly more intentional camera work is earned.

**FOUNDER:**
> "Here's the part I actually want you to remember. We are never taking venture capital on this. There's no VC round two years from now that quietly adds a subscription fee to stay alive. There's no board meeting where somebody decides the open-source license was a mistake."
>
> "If this company folds tomorrow — the boat you bought this for still runs it. The files are already out there. Nobody can take that back."

#### [1:45–2:10] The ask

**VISUAL:** Founder relaxes posture slightly — this section should feel less like a speech and more like a person talking to another boat owner.

**FOUNDER:**
> "We've got three ways to back this. Buy the parts and build it yourself for ninety-nine bucks. Buy a kit and put it together in an afternoon. Or buy it finished, tested, ready to bolt to your helm for less than a third of what the big brands charge for the same job."
>
> "And if you just want to help this exist without needing the hardware — there's a Founders Circle for that too. No equity. Just — thank you, basically."

#### [2:10–2:30] Close — no hard sell, no confetti

**VISUAL:** Wide-ish shot if possible, boat continuing underway, founder either looking back at the water or straight to camera — either is fine, don't over-direct this last beat.

**FOUNDER:**
> "I built this because I was tired of paying rent on a map. If you're tired of it too — back the project, or just go pull the schematics and build your own. Either way, it's yours now."

**[END CARD — text only, no voiceover, no music]**
> OPENPLOTTER
> The Marine Chartplotter You Actually Own
> [Back this project]

#### Notes for editing

- **No score.** If ambient engine/water sound is present, keep it low in the mix rather than replacing it with music — the ambient sound is doing brand work here.
- **Captions:** Burn in captions in JetBrains Mono, small, bottom-third, off-white on a low-opacity charcoal bar — consistent with the brand's monospace-for-facts rule, since this is effectively a factual pitch delivered conversationally.
- **Thumbnail:** Pull a still from the 1:15–1:45 pledge section (the push-in beat) rather than the cold open — it reads as the most committed, least "performing for a camera" frame in the video.
- **Length flexibility:** If Kickstarter platform guidance favors a shorter video, cut the "ask" section down to one tier mention (Full Kit) rather than trimming the pledge section — the pledge is the differentiator; the pricing tiers are already covered elsewhere on the page.
