## Substack Launch Plan — A Plain of Jars

Status: **BUILT 2026-09-19.** Working files live at `/home/jd/Prototypes/Graphic Novel/substack-launch/` (this note is the vault mirror + pointer).

### The strategy in one paragraph

Substack is the top of the funnel for the Kickstarter, not the business itself. Six chapters are drafted in JD's voice (the launch sprint), published weekly (Sundays 9 AM CT), all free while building. The newsletter proves demand, builds the artist-outreach credibility base, and serializes the an unwritten arc (The Road Home) as a way to develop it. At weeks 10-12 the turn: Kickstarter pre-launch page → $40-55k campaign. Realistic numbers: 500-1,000 subs by month 4 is a win; paid tier ($5/mo) at 2-5% conversion = $50-175/mo (tip jar, not income).

### Launch sequence (12 weeks)

W1-6: six drafted posts. W7-8: Road Home parts 1-2 (requires JD's voice memos — never fabricate). W9: "the 15 names" evergreen. W10: artist-sample announcement. W11: KS teaser. W12: KS pre-launch page live + weekly CTA.

### Hard rules enforced (series-notes.md)

- Never narrate Nick's interior; the reason stays unnamed.
- RAND/scantron material = texture-not-allegations (own-experience side of the line, always).
- Rick Steves fictionalized, non-libelous. EMA referenced as context only.
- Content notes on trauma posts (04/05/06). Names changed. Everything that matters is true.
- If it doesn't sound like JD, it doesn't ship.
