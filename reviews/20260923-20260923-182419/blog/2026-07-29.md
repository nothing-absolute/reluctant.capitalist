**19:53 - Marine Electronics Product Line Analysis**
Four open-hardware marine product ideas analyzed for Kickstarter viability: (1) BYO-SIM Boat Monitor ($30-40 BOM, sell for $199) attacks the hated subscription model in boating; (2) Open NMEA 2000 Digital Switching Box ($25-35 BOM, sell for $150-200) brings mega-yacht-grade digital switching to small fishing boats; (3) Smart NMEA 2000 Battery Shunt ($20 BOM, sell for $150-250) for lithium trolling motor batteries; (4) Universal Outboard Engine Data Gateway fights artificially inflated OEM cable prices.
Verdict: Start with the digital switching box or the no-subscription monitor — highest margins, lowest manufacturing risk, solve high-anxiety problems. Use that backer list to later pitch OpenPlotter.

**19:55 - BYO-SIM Boat Monitor: Full Kickstarter Plan**

=== COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE ===
- Siren Marine: $300–800 hardware + $22/mo or $225/yr forced subscription
- BRNKL: $895–4,300 hardware + $215/yr or Starlink-based (no sub but huge upfront)
- Both locked ecosystems, no BYO-SIM, no open protocols
- This product attacks the #1 hated thing in boating: forced subscriptions

=== IOT SIM COST ADVANTAGE ===
- Hologram: $1/mo + $0.03/MB (pay-as-you-go, global)
- Twilio/KORE: $2/mo + $0.10/MB
- 1NCE: $14 ONE-TIME for 10 years (500MB + 250 SMS) — game changer for IoT
- User just pops in their own SIM: $2/mo or $14/10yr. No lock-in.

=== BOM ESTIMATE (250-500 unit qty) ===
- ESP32 module: $3–5
- LTE-M modem (SIM7080 or similar): $10–15
- GPS module: $3–5
- Voltage sensing circuit: $1–2
- Float switch interface + opto: $1
- Power regulation (12V boat to 3.3V/5V): $2–3
- IP67 enclosure: $5–8
- PCB + assembly (low-volume contract): $5–8
- Connectors, pigtails, hardware: $3–5
- TOTAL BOM: ~$35–50 (target $42 at 500pcs)

=== COSTS PER UNIT ===

- Assembly + test: $6
- Packaging + inserts: $4
- Shipping (domestic avg): $10
- KS fee (5%): $10 (on $199)
- Payment processing (3%): $6
- Total COGS: ~$78
- Gross margin per unit: ~$121 (61%)
