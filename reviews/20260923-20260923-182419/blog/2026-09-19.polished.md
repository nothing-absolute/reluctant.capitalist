**16:50 - KS financial model: split / Bluesky / 3 volumes / off-ramps (research-backed)**
Answered JD's money question with sourced data → `projects/a-plain-of-jars/kickstarter-financial-model.md`. Key findings:
- **At-funding split ≈ zero**: V1 at $50k gross → ~$4.3k fees (5% KS + 3-5% processing; KS fee page + Fundpop) → ~$44k production (art $27k + lettering/print/fulfillment/marketing/contingency) → ~$1-2k surplus. The split lives in the CONTRACT (net-revenue share on sales + IP), not the campaign. At $70k overfund: ~$7-9k each; at $100k: ~$20k each. Artist baseline reality: $27k art over 9-24mo full-time ≈ below min wage → artist NEEDS 40-50% net-revenue share for a fair/sticky trilogy deal.
- **Reality check**: comics is the highest-success KS category (67-78%) BUT median raise only ~$17.5k (ICv2 2024/25). $30-100k = "killing field" at ~18.6% success (BackerBench). $50k @ ~$40 avg tier ≈ 1,300-1,700 backers; a 500-sub list pre-sells only 10-25.
- **Bluesky = distribution, not funding**: ~43.5M registered (Bsky Transparency Report) but DAU ~3.5-4.5M and mobile MAU down 27% YoY (Similarweb/TechCrunch Aug 2026). NO creator monetization as of Sep 2026 (no ad share/tips/native subs). Realistic: 1-3k followers yr1 → 5-15% of backers. Newsletter remains the engine.
- **3 volumes feasible as fandom strategy, not growth strategy**: V1 ~1,500-2,000 backers, V2 ~1,200-1,800, V3 ~1,000-1,500 (30-50% returner decay [est]), 4-6 years total. Three separate campaigns (a $100k+ one-shot collapses to ~10% success).
- **Off-ramps preserved by design**: (1) V1-only scope, (2) prose POD/ebook collection as the floor if art stalls, (3) full exit = zero cost today, protected later by milestone payments/kill-fee/options-not-obligations/IP terms, (4) newsletter ends with one post, list stays an asset.
- **Verdict: GO for the artifact, not income** — at 3-volume scale this won't pay rent; what it reliably produces is the book.
