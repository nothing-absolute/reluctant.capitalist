#### Quick Profile

**Name:** JD
**Primary Role:** Creator / Entrepreneur / Multi-project ADHD brain
**Neurotype:** ADHD (neurodivergent)

**One-Sentence Summary:**
Creative polymath with 18+ concurrent ideas, building systems to capture, organize, and actually finish the things that matter — while respecting the ADHD brain's need for novelty and hyperfocus.

#### Work Schedule & Rhythms

##### Energy Patterns
- **Peak Productivity:** Hyperfocus-driven (variable timing)
- **Focus Style:** Interest-based, deep dives on exciting topics
- **Challenge:** The "boring parts" with learning curves get abandoned
- **Strength:** Can see the finished product clearly in mind

##### Planning Preferences
- **Structure vs Flexibility:** Needs external structure but resists rigid systems
- **Daily Plan Style:** Sprint-based timed work sessions with goals
- **Key Need:** Birdseye view of ALL projects in one place

#### Communication & Assistant Preferences

##### Interaction Style

**Preferred Style:**
- Quick, direct suggestions
- ADHD-friendly: micro-tasks, visible progress, dopamine hits
- Gentle "parakeet" reminders, never shame-based

##### Assistant Behavior Guidelines

**When Daily Planning:**
- Check goals to determine "high priority"
- Break overwhelming tasks into micro-chunks (15-30 min max)
- Leverage hyperfocus when it happens
- Minimize context-switching costs (each switch = 30-45 min lost)

**When Working Together:**
- Celebrate small wins publicly
- Track progress visually
- Offer to capture scattered notes/ideas when they appear
- Build recovery time after hyperfocus sessions
