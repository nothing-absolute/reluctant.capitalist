## Vault Dashboard

PARA-organized overview of the vault. Boxes are colored by PARA category
(Projects = orange, Areas = blue, Resources = green, Archive = gray), plus
Daily Plans (yellow), Meeting Notes (teal), Permanent Notes (violet), and the
Map of Content hub (purple). Arrows show the workflow: daily/meeting capture
feeds Areas and Projects, active work gets indexed by the MOC, insights get
promoted into Resources, and everything retires into Archive. Each box has a
link to its matching folder note — click a shape in Excalidraw view to jump
there (edit the `...` link in the raw JSON below if your index notes use
different names than the folder).
