### UNDERTONE — Pitch Deck

**Sub-Harmonic Audio Monitoring & Low-Stimulus Companion Hardware**

#### Slide 1: Executive Overview

##### UNDERTONE — Hear What You Can't. Control What You See.
*A dual-platform hardware and software ecosystem built to protect human wellbeing from sub-harmonic noise pollution and hyper-stimulating screen overload.*

- **SaaS / VST Software**: Real-time 0Hz–20Hz infrasound interception & filtering for desktop audio.
- **Embedded Hardware**: Pass-through SBC device with low-power E-Ink & LCD screens.
- **E-Ink Companion Phone Case**: Digital minimalism, open-source hacker screen, and AOSP parental control lockdown.

#### Slide 2: The Problem

##### 1. Inaudible Sub-Harmonic Noise Pollution
- High-power bass, HVAC systems, industrial equipment, and low-frequency rumble generate sub-harmonics below 20Hz (infrasound).
- Infrasound induces unseen physical fatigue, anxiety, headaches, and sleep disruption without the listener realizing the cause.

##### 2. Hyper-Stimulating Screen Fatigue
- Modern smartphones emit high-density OLED light designed to maximize attention capture and dopamine cycles.
- Existing "dumbphones" force users to compromise on essential utilities (navigation, messaging, high-quality camera).
