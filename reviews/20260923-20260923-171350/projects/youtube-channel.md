### Video Concepts (from yt.notes.txt)

#### Concept 1: Hidden Generation
**Title angle:** "I just discovered a hidden generation covered up for 40 years, and I am ONE OF THEM!!!"
**Focus:** The Xennial generation - early adopters/trailblazers/beta testers of digital media transition
**Key points:**
- Last generation to remember analog-only world
- First to experience digital explosion
- Born 1977-1983 (micro-generation between Gen X and Millennial)
- Could see the future was exciting but individual career paths in arts/media were extremely limited

#### Concept 2: Creekers / Saddle Creek Music Scene
**Title angle:** "The best forgotten indie label and the community that built it"
**Focus:** Saddle Creek Road, Omaha NE indie music scene
**Key points:** Community of musicians, underground movement, overlooked by mainstream

#### Concept 3: Growing Up in South Dakota
**Title angle:** "12 channels and a world of curiosity"
**Focus:** Small town life, limited media access, the hunger to learn about the outside world
**Key points:** Born in Tennessee, moved to South Dakota, Sioux Falls < 100k people

#### Concept 4: Neurodivergent Trailblazers
**Title angle:** "The unrecognized introverts who built the internet"
**Focus:** How ADHD/neurodivergent minds shaped early digital culture
**Key points:** Open minds, curious, built things because they had to

#### Concept 5: Analog to Digital
**Title angle:** "I remember when it was only analog for the masses"
**Focus:** Documenting the transition from analog to digital media
**Key points:** Personal stories of watching the world change
