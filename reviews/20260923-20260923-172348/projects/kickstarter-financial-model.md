## Kickstarter Financial Model — A Plain of Jars

*Modeled 2026-09-19 from KS fee data, comics-category benchmarks (ICv2, Publishers Weekly/BackerBench), Bluesky 2026 stats, and the bible's $40-55k V1 goal. All derived numbers [estimate] unless cited.*

### 1. The split if V1 funds at target

**Headline: at funding target there is no split — the money pays for the book.** Production absorbs it. The share conversation belongs in the contract (revenue on sales + IP), not the campaign take.

| Scenario | Gross | Fees (~8-10%; 5% KS + 3-5% processing) | Production | Surplus | 50/50 split ea | Artist total (incl. $27k art fee) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| At target $50k | $50,000 | ~$4,300 | ~$44k | ~$1-2k | ~$600 | ~$27,600 |
| Overfund $70k | $70,000 | ~$6,000 | ~$50k (+stretch) | ~$14-18k | ~$7-9k | ~$35k |
| $100k | $100,000 | ~$8,500 | ~$50-55k | ~$40k | ~$20k | ~$47k |

Fees: Kickstarter 5% + processing 3-5% (~8-10% total; ~$4-5k on $50k) — kickstarter.com/help/fees, fundpop.co. Production = art $27,000 ($150/page × 180pp, bible) + lettering/design ~$3.5k + print ~$4k/600 copies + pledge manager + fulfillment ~$2k + marketing ~$2.5k + contingency ~$2.5k [estimates]. Taxes: KS applies IRS backup withholding (28% gross, US) = prepaid tax; delay cash flow, not a true fee [verify].

**Reality benchmark:** avg successful solo comics KS nets the creator $10-15k after fees+print for ~5 months' work (Haspiel via Publishers Weekly 2026). Also the honest caveat: $27k art over 9-24 months full-time = below-minimum-wage — artist MUST get profit share for a trilogy commitment to be fair/sticky.

**Recommended deal structure:** $150/page guaranteed base + 40-50% of defined net revenue (production/fulfillment/marketing/taxes subtracted in contract) + 25-50% on adaptations. JD: remaining share + IP ownership + ancillary rights. Original pages ownership + everything defined in writing.

### 2. Campaign reality check

- Comics = highest-success KS category: ~67-78% (ICv2 2024/2025), but median successful comics raise is only ~$17,500. $40-55k = 2.5-3× median → needs 2× median audience or premium tiers.
- Publishing $30k-100k band = "the killing field": ~18.6% success (BackerBench, n=40.6k). $100k+ collapses to ~10.6%.
- $50k at ~$40 avg tier ≈ **1,300-1,700 backers**. Pre-launch list of 500 converts only 2-5% (10-25 backers) — the campaign is won in the month, not pre-sold by the list.

### 3. Bluesky + all-3-volumes feasibility

**Bluesky is distribution, not funding.** Data: ~43.5M registered (Apr 2026, Bluesky 2025 Transparency Report), but DAU only ~3.5-4.5M and mobile MAU ~10.4M, DOWN 27% YoY (Similarweb via TechCrunch, Aug 2026). NO creator monetization as of Sep 2026 — no ad share, no tips, no native subs (Bluesky+ still unlaunched; affiliate model announced, not shipped). Links are NOT downranked (2M clicks/day to publishers). Good for: niche community, image-first comics crowd, cheap channel. Realistic: 1-3k followers yr 1, 5k yr 2 → 5-15% of campaign backers. Strategy remains the newsletter.

**3-volume math [estimates]:** Each volume needs its own campaign + audience growth (returning-backer rate ~30-50% [estimate]). V1 ~1,500-2,000 backers; V2 ~1,200-1,800; V3 ~1,000-1,500 (core only). Timeline: 4-6 years total. Feasible as a FANDOM strategy (deep, small, repeat-paying), not a growth/momentum strategy. Do NOT one-shot a $100k+ trilogy campaign (success ~10%). Three campaigns; V2/V3 as options.

### 4. Off-ramps (all preserved by design)

1. **Scope:** V1 is standalone → stop after V1 cleanly.
2. **Format:** prose serialization = POD/ebook collection (~free marginal cost) if art stalls or KS fails. GN always becomes the "illustrated edition." Floor on all downside.
3. **Full exit:** no artist contract signed yet = zero cost today. After signing, protect via: milestone payments, kill fee, V2/V3 as options NOT obligations, IP/original-art terms, separation clauses.
4. **Newsletter:** one "series pauses here" post ends it; list stays an asset (email "V2 live" later).

**Bottom line: GO for the right reason (the artifact). Not income — at 3-volume scale this won't pay rent. The thing it reliably produces is the book.**
