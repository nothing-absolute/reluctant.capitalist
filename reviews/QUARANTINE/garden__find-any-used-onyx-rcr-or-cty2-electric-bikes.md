### Important Tips When Buying a Used Onyx:
*   **Battery Health is Everything:** The battery is the single most expensive component. Ask the seller how the battery was stored. If it was left at 100% or 0% for long periods, it may have severe degradation. Ask for range estimates.
*   **Check the Company Status:** Keep in mind that Onyx Motorbikes recently faced severe business challenges and halted operations. Because of this, buying a used Onyx means you will largely rely on the community and third-party parts (like from AliExpress or custom builders) for repairs and maintenance rather than official warranty support.
*   **Verify Modifications:** Many RCRs and CTY2s are heavily modified (e.g., aftermarket controllers, Chi batteries). Ask for documentation or receipts for these upgrades.
*   **Test Ride:** Always test the bike in person. Make sure all speed modes work, the brakes (both mechanical and regenerative) function properly, and there are no strange noises from the rear hub motor.
